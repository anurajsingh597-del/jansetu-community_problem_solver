Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for citizens, colleges and industry partners.

How do I submit a community problem?

Go to the Submit Problem section, add the location, category and details, and attach photos if you have them. You'll get a tracking ID instantly.

Who can join as an Industry Partner?

Any registered company, startup or organisation can sign up to mentor student teams, sponsor pilots and help scale verified community solutions.

How do Partner Universities & colleges participate?

Institutions can browse open challenges, build student teams and submit technology proposals directly from the College Dashboard.

Where can I see the proposed Solutions for a problem?

Every tracked problem shows its matched team, proposed solution and pilot status under the Track Problem section.

How do I reach Contact Support?

Email jansetuasplatformname978@gmail.com or call our helpline in the footer below — our team usually replies within 24 hours.
