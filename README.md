# 🇮🇳 JanSetu — Community Problem Solving Platform

### *The Setu Between Citizens & Solutions*

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://jansetu-community-problem-solver.vercel.app/

JanSetu is a **community problem-solving and civic innovation platform** that connects citizens with **colleges, student teams, researchers, government stakeholders and industry partners** to turn real-world community problems into collaborative solutions.

The platform allows citizens to report problems, track their progress, and connect verified challenges with organizations that can contribute technical expertise, resources, mentorship or implementation support.

---

## 🎯 Vision

> **Problems around us. Solutions together.**

Many community problems are identified by citizens, while the required expertise, technology and resources may exist separately within colleges, universities, industries and other organizations.

**JanSetu aims to create a bridge between these stakeholders.**

```text
Citizen
   ↓
Report Problem
   ↓
Verification & Priority
   ↓
College / University + Industry
   ↓
Collaborative Solution
   ↓
Pilot / Implementation
   ↓
Impact Tracking
```

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 Citizen Portal

Citizens can:

* Submit community problems
* Add problem title and detailed description
* Select category and urgency
* Add location and district
* Mention approximate number of affected people
* Upload photos, videos and supporting evidence
* Add their own solution idea
* Track submitted problems
* Support, like and follow challenges
* Provide feedback

---

### 🗺️ Interactive Problem Map

The platform provides an interactive map concept for discovering community problems.

Problems can be filtered by categories such as:

* 🚨 Disaster
* 💧 Water
* 🎓 Education
* 🏥 Healthcare
* 🌾 Agriculture

Users can discover problems based on **location, category and urgency**.

---

### 📊 Priority Score

JanSetu introduces a transparent priority mechanism to help identify high-impact problems.

The priority score considers signals such as:

* Urgency
* Number of affected people
* Severity
* Community support

Example:

**Priority Score: 92/100**

This helps solution teams and stakeholders focus on problems that may require greater attention.

---

### 🔎 Problem Tracking

Every submitted problem can have a tracking ID.

Example:

```text
JS-2026-1048
```

Citizens can use the ID to check the current status of their problem and understand where it has reached.

## 🤝 Collaborative Solution Workspace

Once a problem is matched with a solution team, stakeholders can collaborate through a common workspace.

The workspace can contain:

* Tasks
* Deadlines
* Team members
* Progress tracking
* Field reviews
* Technical prototypes
* Industry mentorship
* Citizen feedback
* Team communication

This creates a collaborative workflow between **citizens, students, faculty and industry mentors**.

---

## 📈 Impact Tracking

JanSetu doesn't stop at reporting a problem.

The platform also proposes tracking the outcome of solutions through metrics such as:

* Number of beneficiaries
* Risk reduction
* Community feedback
* Pilot status
* Implementation progress
* Project impact

This helps shift the focus from simply **reporting problems** to **measuring solutions**.

## 💡 Example Use Case — Flood Management

Consider a village school that becomes inaccessible during heavy rainfall.

### Step 1 — Citizen

A citizen reports:

> Flooding near village school

They provide:

* Location
* Photos/videos
* Number of affected people
* Urgency
* Problem description

### Step 2 — Verification & Priority

The problem receives a priority score based on its severity and impact.

### Step 3 — College

A college creates a student team to work on:

* Flood mapping
* Sensor prototype
* Safe-access planning

### Step 4 — Industry

An industry partner can provide:

* Mentorship
* Technology
* Funding
* Implementation support

### Step 5 — Collaboration

All stakeholders coordinate through the collaborative workspace.

### Step 6 — Impact

The implemented solution can be monitored through impact metrics.
